<p>Accomplish helps you track and finish everything on your plate, and keeps two kinds of task apart: big, important ones on Home in a fixed number of spots, and everyday small ones, one tap away and out of sight until you want them. They're called IMP and Small stuff by default, and you can rename both.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>One next step at a time.</b> Each big task shows its next step, and a meter shows roughly how much is left.</li>
<li><b>Frost.</b> Big tasks you leave untouched slowly frost over. One tap thaws them.</li>
<li><b>Deadlines or a rough when.</b> A date, "This month" or "Later". Anything due tomorrow or sooner turns red, and Coming up shows the next six weeks.</li>
<li><b>Reminders with snooze</b>, plus optional nudges before deadlines, with quiet hours and a daily limit.</li>
<li><b>A Waiting room</b> for big tasks that aren't on Home yet. Drag cards on Home to reorder them.</li>
<li><b>Capsules.</b> Moving a big task forward sometimes earns one: a note from your past self, a real-life side quest, or a one-minute lesson.</li>
<li><b>Undo</b> for anything that can't simply be put back, and a full history for every task.</li>
<li><b>Make it yours:</b> choose the names, the number of spots, the timings, the notifications and the theme.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Private by design.</b> No internet permission, no accounts, no analytics. Your tasks are a readable JSON file on your SD card or phone storage, with daily backups.</p>
